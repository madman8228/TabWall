TabWall Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026

What TabWall does

TabWall saves browser tabs into a local tab wall so you can organize and restore them later.

Information stored

When you use TabWall, it may store the page title, URL, favicon URL, tab order, access time, save time, and TabWall display preferences. This information is stored locally in the browser using the extension's IndexedDB and storage areas.

How information is used

The information is used only to display, organize, and restore your saved tabs. TabWall does not send this information to a server, use analytics, sell it, or share it with third parties. TabWall does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization.

InPrivate browsing

If you allow TabWall to run in an InPrivate window, TabWall keeps InPrivate sessions separate from regular browser sessions. The saved information is still stored locally until you restore or clear it. Saving a page in InPrivate does not mean that the extension storage is automatically deleted when the InPrivate window closes.

Permissions

  • tabs: read tab information and create or restore browser tabs.
  • storage: save TabWall sessions and preferences locally.
  • contextMenus: provide the right-click action to save the current page.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when TabWall's functionality changes. For support, please visit TabWall support and feedback.
